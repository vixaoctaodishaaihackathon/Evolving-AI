Topic:
How can Organizations construct self evolving Ai agents network to automatically manage complex , real time global supply chains and predict market shift.
Orchestration:
LangGraph or Temporal.io critical for durable execution and human in the loop fallback on high risk supplier contracts.
Streaming and Storage:
Apache Kafka/Redpanda for live event ingestions; Clickhouse for time series analytics; Neo4j or Memgraph for the tier-N supplier knowledge graph.
Observability And Guardrails:
Arize Phoenix or Langfuse for tracing ; Nemo Guardrails to constrain agent spend and operational limits.
Role:
Spin up the core agent architecture (LangGraph/Temporal Work Flows) configure Docker Containers for Kafka/ClickHouse and write the ingestion pipeline scripts.
